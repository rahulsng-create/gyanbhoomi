Privacy Policy

Clear information about what GyanBhoomi collects and how it is used.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: We ask only for information needed to create and operate your account and improve learning features you choose to use. We do not need your password in order to personalise your experience. Personalisation is optional and can be turned off.

1. Information we may collect

  • Account details: name, email address and the password you create. Password authentication is handled by Supabase Auth; GyanBhoomi's page does not intentionally save your password in its personalisation records.
  • Profile details: date of birth, country, state or province, city, postal code, mobile number and preferred language when you provide them during registration.
  • Learning and preference data: only if you allow personalisation, your preferred language, saved or favourite articles, reading activity, search topics and last-visit time may be remembered to suggest relevant learning content.
  • Technical information: hosting, authentication and security providers may process basic technical information such as connection, device or error logs to deliver and protect their services.

2. Why we use it

  • To create, authenticate and secure your account.
  • To provide account recovery and important account messages.
  • To remember profile settings such as your chosen language.
  • If you opt in, to personalise article and learning recommendations.
  • To operate, troubleshoot and protect GyanBhoomi and prevent misuse.

We do not treat consent to one purpose as permission for unrelated future uses. If a new feature needs personal information for a new purpose, we should explain that purpose and request consent where required.

3. Personalisation is optional

The personalisation prompt offers separate choices: “Allow personalisation” or “Not now”. If you allow it, GyanBhoomi remembers selected learning preferences in your browser and may sync the personalisation summary to your signed-in Supabase account. You can switch personalisation off or clear the saved browser data using the Personalisation controls in Settings. Turning it off stops new personalisation collection; use the clear option to remove the saved personalisation data from that browser.

4. Passwords and account security

Your password is used for authentication through Supabase Auth. Do not share it with anyone. GyanBhoomi's personalisation feature does not record passwords, private messages or keystrokes. Use a unique password and sign out on shared devices.

5. Services that help operate the site

  • Supabase: account authentication and related account data.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting and delivery of the website.
  • Google: only if you choose the Google sign-in option; Google's own privacy terms also apply.
  • Browser storage: local storage is used for your personalisation choice and, if allowed, learning preference data.

External websites linked from GyanBhoomi have their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

6. How long information is kept

Account information is kept while needed to provide your account and comply with applicable requirements. Browser personalisation information remains until you clear it or remove the site data. A specific account-deletion and data-request process must be provided by the site operator; contact details for that process must be published before launch.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can choose not to allow optional personalisation.
  • You can turn personalisation off and clear its saved browser data in Settings.
  • You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing, subject to applicable law.
  • You can stop using the service and sign out at any time.

For privacy requests, the site operator must publish a working privacy contact channel. GyanBhoomi's official privacy contact email has not yet been supplied/configured. Please add one before treating this policy as final.

8. Children

GyanBhoomi includes educational material that may be viewed by children. If an account is created for a person under 18, the site operator must implement the legally required parent or guardian consent and child-safety safeguards before collecting or using that child's personal information. Do not enter a child's personal details unless the required permission has been obtained.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or applicable law changes. The latest version and its update date will be shown on this page. Material changes should be brought to users' attention.

10. Legal framework

GyanBhoomi is intended to handle personal information responsibly and to align with applicable Indian privacy law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, as their provisions come into force. This notice is an operational draft, not a legal opinion or a certification of compliance.

Official references: Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 · Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025.
