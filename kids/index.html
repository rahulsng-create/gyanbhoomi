🎵 Picture poems & rhymes

Read aloud, clap the rhythm and enjoy the pictures.

🌙 Little Moon

Hello, Moon, up in the sky,
Silver stars are shining high.
Close my eyes and drift away,
Dream of stars till break of day.

Clap once for every line!

🐦 Little Bird

Little bird upon the tree,
Sing a happy song for me.
Flap your wings and touch the sky,
Wave hello as you fly by!

Can you make a gentle bird sound?

📚 Stories with pictures

Short read-along stories with a lesson to remember.

🐢 The Tortoise and the Rabbit

Rabbit laughed at Tortoise for being slow. They raced. Rabbit ran far ahead and took a nap. Tortoise kept walking, step by step, and reached the finish first.

Lesson: Keep trying and do not give up.

🌱 The Little Seed

A child planted a tiny seed and watered it every day. Soon a green shoot appeared, then leaves, and finally a bright flower.

Lesson: Good things grow with patience and care.

A tiny story break

Take a moment for a little tale about kindness and courage.
