GyanBhoomi is a concept for an open, multilingual knowledge library. The goal is to preserve useful information, make it easy to discover, and help contributors improve it responsibly.

Quality promise: articles should cite trustworthy sources, show when they were updated, distinguish facts from opinions, and preserve revision history.
